**Black ICE Chinese v1.2.1** is available. This update makes game information easier to read and offers a full package for players installing the mod for the first time. You will need a legitimate copy of *Hearts of Iron III: Their Finest Hour* 4.02. [Download the full package and read the installation instructions](https://github.com/Zealoter/BlackICE-Chinese-Releases/releases/tag/v1.2.1)

## New features

- **Scrollable country modifiers.** The flag panel groups national modifiers by source. Move the pointer back to the map to resume zooming with the mouse wheel.
- **Technology details on hover.** The table shows effects per level, cumulative effects, research requirements, and expandable lists of affected unit types. Short tables shrink to fit. The level selector changes the preview only.

## Fixes

- **Battle marker numbers.** The map indicators use a bitmap font at both zoom levels. I confirmed in-game that numbers on the red and green markers display correctly.
- **Missing characters.** The update fills gaps in province names such as Xingtai and in rotated Chinese text.
- **Building tooltips and leader progress.** Building tooltips use proportional lettering for labels such as `AG 防空火炮`. Leader trait progress uses the same text in both localization layers to address trailing question marks. I welcome further reports on both changes.

## Next up

- Restore red text when allocated IC falls short of demand, starting with reinforcements.
- Improve the hierarchy of battle information and fix text hidden below corps headquarters in the unit panel.
- Audit missing leader portraits. Use sourced historical photographs where available and label AI-generated placeholders as illustrations.
- Review the IC thresholds for minor-country research bonuses. I would like weaker industrial economies to research more slowly on their own while allowing them to pay for advanced technology. This remains a proposal for a separate balance pass.

This is a free, unofficial localization and interface project. The package credits the Black ICE team, the original Chinese localization team, and translation contributors.
